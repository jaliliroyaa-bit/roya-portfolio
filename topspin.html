TOPSPIN

TOPSPIN is a visual identity and packaging project developed as a cohesive brand system across multiple touchpoints.

The project includes logo development, color palette, illustration, stationery, packaging, stickers and digital applications, with a consistent visual language designed to keep the brand recognizable across both print and digital media.
