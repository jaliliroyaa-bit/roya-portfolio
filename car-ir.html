Selected Project

CAR.IR

Visual Identity · Brand System · Digital Experience

Overview

A unified identity for an automotive media platform.

CAR.IR is an automotive media platform focused on delivering automotive news, reviews, tests and digital content.

The project focused on developing and refining a cohesive visual identity system across different brand touchpoints — from logo and typography to stationery, reporter apparel, social media and the digital experience.

The goal was to create a recognizable and flexible system that could remain consistent across both editorial and commercial applications.
