Brand Overview

Mahboubeh

Mahboubeh is an occupational therapy clinic primarily focused on working with children. The visual identity was designed to create a friendly and approachable presence while maintaining the professional character of the clinic.

The identity is built around a character-based logo inspired by the therapist herself. The character was developed as a recognizable visual element that could give the clinic a more personal and human presence while remaining simple enough to work consistently across different brand applications.
